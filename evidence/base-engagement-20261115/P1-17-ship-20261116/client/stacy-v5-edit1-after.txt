About 3 days after your first assessment, ROMBot may leave you one check-in note in the app. Depending on how many sessions you have logged, the note uses some of these details from your own account: your first name, how many sessions you have logged since your assessment, today's focus area from your plan and your next check date. We keep the note with your ROMBot chats, linked to the assessment it is about. If you turned on reminders and have not logged a session, taken an assessment or messaged ROMBot since the note, we may also send you one email about it on a plan day, at your reminder time. That email goes to your email address and uses some of the same details.
